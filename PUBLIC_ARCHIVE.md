# Public archive scope

This repository makes the original Kaggriculture research process reviewable
without republishing competition-controlled files, generated submissions, or
third-party notebook copies. The tracked files are first-party source,
evaluators, written research, and lightweight provenance; external material
remains at its source.

Read [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) before reproducing any work. The
release boundary, exclusions, and verification procedure are in
[RELEASE_MANIFEST.md](RELEASE_MANIFEST.md).

Portfolio index: [kaggle-research-portfolio](https://github.com/openkaggle/kaggle-research-portfolio). Related archives: [Nemotron reasoning](https://github.com/openkaggle/nemotron-reasoning-research), [NeuroGolf ONNX](https://github.com/openkaggle/neurogolf-2026-onnx-research), and [Maze Crawler](https://github.com/openkaggle/maze-crawler-research).
