# Kaggriculture Research

An OpenKaggle research workbench for reading, testing, and discussing
competition-agent methods. It keeps the parts of the work that can be shared
and reviewed—original source, evaluators, build tools, and research notes—while
keeping competition-controlled and third-party material at its original source.

This archive is not a submission client. Publishing it does not authorize a
Kaggle upload, an active-slot change, or a new remote experiment.

## What's here

- Original agents, builders, verifiers, and local evaluation utilities.
- Campaign notes, reproducibility guidance, and lightweight provenance.
- A clear boundary for files that must stay out of a public source archive.

## What's deliberately not here

- Kaggriculture downloads, replays, cache output, submissions, and credentials.
- `actions.json` policy fixtures and `tapes.json` replay-derived fixtures.
- Third-party Kaggle notebooks, kernels, and copied metadata.

Use the official sources listed in [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) rather
than a mirror in this repository. The exact public-release inventory and its
checks are recorded in [RELEASE_MANIFEST.md](RELEASE_MANIFEST.md); licensing
boundaries are in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

For the broader archive, see [PUBLIC_ARCHIVE.md](PUBLIC_ARCHIVE.md).
